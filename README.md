# My Favorite Movie Series: *Marvel Cinematic Universe (2010–2019)*

The **Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU)** is my all-time favorite movie series. From 2010 to 2019, Marvel released **23 interconnected superhero films** that built up to the *Infinity Saga*, one of the greatest storytelling achievements in cinema history. ## Why I Love the MCU
- Every movie connects to a bigger story, I really love the feeling of watching puzzle pieces come together.  
- The characters are unforgettable, each with their own struggles and growth. I honestly loved everyone. (especially Loki hehe)
- The balance of **action, humor, and emotion** keeps me hooked every time. Also because of America's ass ;)
